🎯 What this map is for

This is your live directory of all active third-party technicians across the US. Use it to find the right tech for any job, check coverage in any area, estimate travel costs, and keep tech contact info up to date — all in one place.

⚙ Quick Start

1

Browse the map — pins are color-coded by Internal Rating. Click any pin to see contact info.

2

Find the nearest tech — type a city or ZIP in the "Find Nearest Techs" bar and hit Find Nearest.

3

Filter your view — use the rating buttons and territory dropdown to narrow the list.

4

Check coverage gaps — click ⚠ Gap Analysis to see which states need techs.

5

Update tech info — click any pin and use "Suggest an Edit" to submit changes for review.

💡 Tip: This map requires an internet connection to load the map tiles. All tech data is embedded and available offline.

📍 Understanding the Pins

Each pin represents one active technician. The number inside the pin is their Internal Rating. Click any pin to open their full contact card.

⭐ Internal Rating System

1 – Top Technician — Highest confidence. Go-to for critical or complex jobs. Dispatch first.

2 – Good — Solid and reliable. Handles most work well with standard oversight.

3 – Needs Support — Can get the job done but may need guidance. Use as backup.

4 – Struggling — Use with caution. Monitor closely or avoid for complex jobs.

📞 Contact Card

Click any pin to see the full contact card including name, company, phone (tap to call), email (tap to open), territory, travel radius, and internal notes. Use "Suggest an Edit" at the bottom of the card to propose any updates.

💡 Tip: Pins close together will be slightly offset on the map so they're all clickable. Zoom in to separate them.

🔍 Search Bar

Type any part of a tech's name, company, city, or state. The map updates instantly showing only matching techs. Clear the box to show all.

🌎 Territory Filter

Use the dropdown to filter by territory — Central US, East Coast, or West Coast. Combined with the search bar, this narrows your list quickly for regional dispatch decisions.

⬤ Rating Filter Buttons

Click Top Tech, Good, or Support to show only techs at that rating level. Click All to reset. The badges in the header update to show counts for your current view.

💡 Tip: Combine filters — for example, filter to "Top Tech" + "East Coast" to quickly find your best East Coast resources.

📍 Find Nearest Techs

1

Type a city name, full address, or ZIP code in the Find Nearest Techs bar at the top.

2

Select Top 3, 5, or 10 from the dropdown.

3

Click Find Nearest or press Enter.

4

The map zooms to show a red dot at your location with dashed blue lines to the nearest techs.

5

A results panel slides in on the right showing techs ranked by distance. Click any card to fly to that tech on the map.

6

Click ✕ Clear to reset back to the full map.

🚘 Travel Cost Estimates

Each result shows estimated drive time, one-way cost, and round-trip cost calculated at $0.76/mile. Round-trip cost turns red ⚠ when it exceeds $200 to flag higher-cost dispatches.

💡 Tip: Distance is straight-line (as the crow flies). Actual drive time may vary based on roads. Use as a relative comparison, not an exact ETA.

◯ Coverage Radius

Click the ⊙ Radius button in the toolbar to show each tech's preferred travel range as a dashed circle on the map. The circle color matches their rating tier. Toggle off to hide all circles.

💡 Use Radius + Find Nearest together to confirm a tech's circle actually covers the job site before dispatching.

⚠ Gap Analysis

Click ⚠ Gap Analysis to open a side panel that grades every US state by coverage level:

No Coverage — Zero techs in this state. Priority recruit target.

Low Coverage — Only 1 tech, no Top Tech. Vulnerable if that tech is unavailable.

Covered — 2+ techs or at least one Top Tech. Good redundancy.

Gap Analysis updates live based on your current filters — for example, filter to Top Tech only to see which states have zero top-rated coverage.

+ Add New Tech

1

Click + Add Tech in the toolbar.

2

Fill in the tech's name, contact info, rating, territory, and travel range.

3

Click Submit for Review. The tech goes into the review queue — they are NOT live on the map yet.

4

Open the Review Panel to approve or reject the submission.

✎ Suggest an Edit

1

Click any pin to open the contact card.

2

Click ✎ Suggest an Edit at the bottom of the card.

3

Update any fields — phone, email, rating, notes, etc.

4

Click Submit for Review. Changes go to the review queue, not live immediately.

📋 Review Panel

Click 📋 Review in the toolbar (shows a red badge when items are pending). Review each submission and click ✓ Approve or ✗ Reject.

⬇ Export to CSV for Salesforce

After approving submissions, click ⬇ Export to CSV for Salesforce in the Review Panel. This downloads a formatted CSV file with all approved new techs and edits, ready to import directly into Salesforce contacts. The export clears the approved queue automatically.

💡 Tip: The review queue saves in your browser between sessions. Export regularly so approved changes get into Salesforce and aren't lost if you clear your browser data.
